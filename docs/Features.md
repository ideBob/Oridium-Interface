# Features

Full list of features included in Oridium Interface.

---

## Combat

### Aimbot
- Smooth camera lock onto the closest player inside FOV
- Default keybind: **Q**
- Adjustable smoothness
- Visual FOV Circle, Snapline, and Head Dot

### Silent Aim
- Redirects bullets toward the target without moving your camera
- Uses `__namecall` hook on Raycast and older ray methods
- Automatically finds the closest target under your mouse

### Triggerbot
- Automatically shoots when a player enters your mouse FOV
- Equips Revolver / Double Barrel automatically
- Adjustable FOV size
- Built-in cooldown for more natural shooting

---

## Visuals

### ESP
- Box ESP
- Health Bar (changes color based on HP)
- Distance display
- Customizable color
- Default keybind: **M**

### Aimviewer
- Shows where other players are looking with a red line

---

## Player

### High Jump
- Increases your JumpPower
- Slider from 50 to 300
- Automatically reapplies after respawn

### Anti Detection
- Basic spoofing of JumpPower and WalkSpeed reads
- Helps against simple client-side checks

---

## Good / Bad Boys System

- Select any player from the list
- **Sign Good Boys** → Green cross (✝) appears above their head
- **Sign Bad Boys** → Red cross (†) appears above their head
- Marks follow the player and persist after respawn
- Lists can be cleared anytime

---

## UI

- Clean Linoria-based interface
- **RGB Mode** – Makes the accent color cycle through the rainbow
- Config saving (settings persist between sessions)
- Custom thunderbolt intro animation
- Toggle UI with **End** key
